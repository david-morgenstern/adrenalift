Performance

The settings that actually move performance on RDNA4. Change one thing at a time and test stability. Everything lives in RAM only — a reboot restores stock values.

Power limit

The most reliable win: give the GPU a little more power budget so it holds its boost clock longer. Works without a scan.

Stock reference is ~330 W; ~340 W is a safe nudge.

GFX clock offset

A fixed MHz shift on the graphics clock (OverDrive). Needs a deep scan first (Boost Clock tab → tick the deep-scan box).

Start small (e.g. +25–50 MHz).

Advanced: OverDrive PPT (% over default)

The percentage-based equivalent of the power limit above. Prefer the absolute watt control unless you specifically want a percentage. Needs a deep scan.
